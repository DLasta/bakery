Our Heritage

Baked With Tradition. Made For Today.

Golden Crust Bakery began with a simple idea: good bread should take time. We combine traditional European baking methods with carefully selected ingredients to create bread and pastries that bring people together.

Every loaf is fermented naturally for up to 36 hours to yield optimum depth of flavor, crisp crust, and light digestive texture.
